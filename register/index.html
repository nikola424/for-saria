What most people get wrong

Funding isn’t luck. It’s a process you can learn.

What you’ve been told
What’s actually true
“Grants are only for nonprofits.”
Corporations and foundations fund for-profit small businesses every year.
“You need investors to grow.”
Grants and pitch prizes are non-dilutive. You keep 100% of your company.
“You have to be a natural on stage.”
You need a clear story and real numbers. A pitch is a skill you can practise.
“Small businesses never win.”
Judges fund people they believe in. Most of the work is how you tell it.
